Telehealth is no longer a temporary solution - it’s a permanent part of healthcare, particularly in behavioral health. By 2026, 85.9% of psychiatrists are conducting video visits, with hybrid care models blending in-person and virtual services becoming the norm. This shift demands advanced EHR systems and patient engagement tools to handle telehealth documentation, billing, and compliance.
