Disconnected systems in behavioral health - where EHR, HR, and patient engagement tools operate separately - are costly and inefficient. Here's why integration matters: 82% of providers use 4+ separate systems, leading to 10–20+ hours/week wasted on redundant tasks. Manual data entry increases errors, causing 32% of billing denials and compliance risks like expired clinician credentials.
